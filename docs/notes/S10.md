# S10: P2 cables (step 15, second part)

## Changelog
### Added
- **Cables** in the power plan (Gig → Tools → Power plan → Cables): each cable run with its type, length, how many alike and how it's laid (spread out, bundled with others, left on a drum) and the air around it. Power cables are linked to a part of the plan (a distro's feed or an output) and show the current they carry, what they may carry laid like that, and the voltage drop added up from the supply: a check above 3 %, over above 5 %. A warning also says when the breaker in front is larger than the cable can carry. DMX, speaker and signal cables are typed with a from and a to.
- "Add cables from the plan" adds one cable for each distro feed and each output in use, to edit.
- **Cable list**: the cables by type and length, matched to catalogue items by name (or picked by hand). Missing items can be created in the catalogue in one tap, and the list goes into the gig's equipment (only what's missing), and so into the packing list.
- **Suggest where to plug in**: balance the phases, or fill each output up to a reserve of its breaker (80 % by default). Every change and the phases before and after are shown before anything is applied; undo after.
- **Inrush at switch-on**: the datasheet inrush of the loads on each output is added up and compared with the breaker; above 3 × it may trip a B-curve breaker, above 5 × a C-curve one.
- **Distro and cable labels** (`/gig/:id/power/labels`): cables 50 × 25 mm, distros 100 × 50 mm, on pre-cut A4 sheets or a roll.
- Everything is labelled as an estimate for a qualified electrician; figures come from a published H07RN-F cable datasheet and cable tables, with the sources on the report.
### Connected
- The power report and its PDF (Save to gig files) add the inrush groups, the cable runs and the cable list, with the method and sources.
- Gig equipment and packing list: the cable list's "Add to the gig's equipment".
- Labels (U5): distro and cable labels in the D31 sizes, from the power plan and the report.
- Help: the "Plan the cables" guide, terms (voltage drop, cable capacity, inrush current) and ⓘ hints.

## Pending: new questions (section 3)
- **Proposed cable set (owner to trim):** power, H07RN-F: Schuko 3G1.5 (3, 5, 10, 20 m), Schuko 3G2.5 (5, 10, 20, 25 m), powerCON 3G2.5 (1, 3, 5, 10 m), powerCON TRUE1 3G2.5 (1, 3, 5, 10 m), CEE 16 A single-phase 3G2.5 (5, 10, 25 m), CEE 16 A three-phase 5G2.5 (5, 10, 25 m), CEE 32 A single-phase 3G6 (10, 25 m), CEE 32 A three-phase 5G6 (5, 10, 25, 50 m), CEE 63 A 5G16 (10, 25, 50 m), CEE 125 A 5G35 (10, 25, 50 m), Powerlock set 5 × 1×95 (10, 25, 50 m), Socapex 19G2.5 (10, 25, 50 m). DMX XLR 5-pin and 3-pin (1, 3, 5, 10, 20 m). Speaker: speakON NL4 2.5 mm² (5, 10, 20 m), NL4 4 mm² (10, 20, 30 m), NL8 2.5 mm² (10, 20 m). Signal: XLR mic/line (3, 5, 10, 20 m), etherCON Cat6 (1, 5, 10, 20, 50 m), BNC 3G-SDI (5, 10, 25, 50 m). Lengths are suggestions in the length box; any length can be typed. Catalogue items are matched by name ("CEE 32 A three-phase (5G6) · 25 m"), so naming your items like that (or picking them once) maps them.
- **Cable data:** one manufacturer's H07RN-F datasheet (General Cavi, 2025-02-06) and Lapp's T12 tables (from DIN VDE 0298-4:2013-06). Is that cable what you stock? Other cables (e.g. H07BQ-F, Titanex) can differ; a per-item cable spec on catalogue items would be a later step. These values need a second person's check like the truss tables (docs/TRUSS_DATA.md).
- **Voltage drop method:** resistance at 60 °C, reactance left out, cos φ 1, drop added up along the chain; limits 3 % (check) and 5 % (over) from TOOLS_PLAN §4. Different limits for lighting and other loads (IEC 60364-5-52 suggests 3 % and 5 %)?
- **Socapex:** 12 loaded cores derate a 19G2.5 to 10 A per circuit (T12 table 12-3, row for 14). This is cautious when not every circuit is loaded. Keep?
- **Inrush thresholds:** the breaker curve isn't known, so 3 × (B curve) and 5 × (C curve) In from IEC 60898-1 bands. Add a curve per output (B/C/D) later?
- **Balancing:** only outputs of connected distros are used, three-phase loads only go on 3P outputs, and the reserve also holds on the distros above and on the supply. Is that right?
- **Labels:** no QR on the power labels (U6 decides QR on prints); U5 can fold these into its templates.
- Created catalogue items are named in the language the editor uses at that moment, counted in bulk, category "Cables"/"Cabos", price 0.
