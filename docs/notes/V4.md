# V4: transport costs, expenses per vehicle, vehicle-use report (step 23, part 4)

## Changelog
### Added
- **Transport cost on each gig**: for every vehicle on a gig's trips, the km × its running cost per km plus the days it drives for the gig × its cost per day. Admins and members see it under the trips on the gig's **Transport** tab, per vehicle, with a warning for trips without km.
- **Gig finance includes transport**: a new "Transport" line in the gig's finance, so the margin (gig panel, Gig margins report and its CSV, Overview tiles, gig cards, wrap-up) includes it. The company's money results (Reports → Money) are unchanged: they add up real expenses.
- **Expenses linked to a vehicle**: fuel, tolls or a repair can be linked to a vehicle and, if you like, one of its trips (the trip fills in its gig).
- **Reports → Vehicle use**: per vehicle and period, trips, gigs, km, days used, load against capacity ("not recorded" for now), running cost, linked expenses and the total; with comparison, chart, details table, CSV and print.
- **Organisation export**: three new files, vehicles, trips and vehicle expenses.
- Help: term "Transport cost", ⓘ hints on the transport cost and the expense's vehicle, guide "See what your vehicles cost". The vehicle form now says to leave fuel out of the cost per km if you log fuel receipts.

### Fixed
- Closing an account no longer fails for someone who added a vehicle, or who made, drove or co-drove a trip (also a removed one).

### Connected
- Gig finance (step 10) through `public.gig_finance`; expenses (9b); Reports (tab and menu item); the organisation export.
- Not connected: load against capacity waits for V3's loads per vehicle (`vehicle_use.load_kg` is returned empty for V3 to fill); K2 `finance_results` deliberately unchanged.
