## Changelog
### Added
- **Appointments**: site visits, meetings, warehouse days and other calendar items that aren't a gig, with a title, place, a day and times (or whole days), notes, and an optional gig or client. Organisation admins and members add them and invite the organisation's members and its gigs' crew; Organisation → Appointments and My work → Appointments list them.
- While you choose people, the form shows who is already booked at that time.
- Help: the "Plan a site visit" guide, the term "Appointment" and two ⓘ hints, in English and Portuguese.

### Connected
- Calendars: appointments appear in My work's and the organisation's calendar, with a clock icon.
- Clashes: people invited are busy for that time, so appointments show in every double-booking warning (with their title to people who may see them, as "booked" to everyone else).
- Calendar feed: your appointments and the trips you drive or co-drive are in your phone's calendar.
- Notifications: invited, moved, cancelled or taken off, in the app, by push and in the email summary, as each person has set.
- The gig's Overview and the client's card list their linked appointments, with "New appointment".

## Pending: test lines (section 2)
- As an admin, add a site visit tomorrow 10:00–11:00 for a member who is on a gig that day: the form warns while you tick them; after saving they get a notification and see it in My work → Appointments and the calendar.
- Move it to 15:00: they're told "moved"; cancel it: they're told and the ⚠ on that gig's crew tab goes away.
- Link one to a gig and to a client: it shows on the gig's Overview and in the client's card.
- Subscribe to the calendar feed and check the appointment (and a trip you drive, once V1 is in) appears.
- Change the organisation's time zone: an all-day appointment stays on the same days.

## Pending: new questions (section 3)
- **Who can be invited?** Assumed: the organisation's members and anyone who has been crew on its gigs. Other options: members only; anyone found through Find crew.
- **Should org viewers see all the organisation's appointments?** Assumed no: they see only the ones they're invited to (planners see all).
- **Should a gig's PM (not an org member) plan appointments linked to their gig?** Assumed no: only the organisation's admins and members.
- **Do invited people answer (accept/decline)?** Not built: they're told and booked, like crew added to a gig.
- **Notifications are not "direct" (a muted organisation mutes them)**, like being added to a gig. Should invitations get through a mute?
