# S11: Step 16, e-signature

## Changelog
### Added
- **Signatures you can't change.** A signature pad for a finger, a pen or the mouse. Each signature
  keeps the drawing, the signer's typed name (and role or company), the time and a fingerprint
  (SHA-256) of the exact document signed, with a signed PDF. Once saved, nobody can change it, not
  even AVX; it can only be hidden. It is a simple electronic signature, not a qualified one (no
  Chave Móvel Digital or Cartão de Cidadão), and the terms say so.
- **Quotes accepted with a drawn signature.** On the quote's page the client can add a drawn
  signature next to their typed name (optional). In "Send to client" the answer shows it, and
  **Signed PDF** makes and keeps the signed quote.
- **Delivery notes signed on site.** Packing list → **Delivery note: sign**: hand the phone to the
  client, who checks what went out, types their name and signs. The signed PDF is kept and listed
  under the packing list.

### Connected
- Quotes (client page and Send to client), packing lists, gig files (each signed PDF is copied
  there), the privacy notice and terms in both languages, the data map, help (glossary
  "Simple electronic signature", an ⓘ on the delivery note, guides "Get a delivery note signed on
  site" and "A quote accepted with a drawn signature").
