# V1: vehicles and trips (step 23, part 1)

## Changelog
### Added
- **Vehicles** (Inventory → Vehicles): your vans and trucks with plate, the driving licence they need, maximum load (kg), load space (m³), running cost per km and per day, and notes. Each shows the trips it's booked for. Removing a vehicle hides it from new trips; old trips keep it.
- **Trips** on a gig (new **Transport** tab): out, back or one way, with a vehicle, a driver from the gig's crew, an optional co-driver and passengers, when it leaves and arrives, from and to, and the km. "Plan the way back too" adds the return in one go. Everyone on the gig sees the trips; the gig's managers and the organisation's admins and members plan them.
- **Clashes for vehicles and drivers**: a vehicle or driver already booked at that time shows a warning while planning and on the trip. Nothing is blocked.
- **Driving licence** (Settings → Profile): tick the categories on your licence. Only you and your organisations' admins see them (admins on the person page). A driver whose licence doesn't include what the vehicle needs, or who hasn't recorded it, gives the planner a warning they can override after checking.
- **Driving hours**: each logged day has "Driving (minutes)" next to travel, filled in from the gig's trips where you drive. Approval stores it as driving hours, paid like travel at the plain hourly rate; totals and the hours CSV show driving apart.
- Help: terms "Vehicle", "Trip", "Licence category", "Driving hours"; ⓘ hints on the vehicle form, the trip's driver, the licence and driving minutes; guide "Add a vehicle and plan a trip".

### Connected
- Reservations engine: new kinds `vehicle`/`trip` and `person`/`trip_driver`, `trip_codriver`. A driver's trip shows where crew bookings already show: other gigs' Crew tab clashes, My work's double bookings, Find crew's "busy".
- Time registration: driving minutes, the `driving` hours in the approved split, cost, totals and CSV.
- Gig page: the Transport tab. Inventory: the Vehicles tab. Person page: the licence. Data map: licences and trips.
- Not connected (other parts of step 23): calendar feed (V2's), packing list per vehicle (V3), transport costs in gig finance, expenses per vehicle and the vehicle-use report (V4).
