# S09: P1 power distribution (step 15, first part)

## Changelog
### Added
- **Power plan** on each gig (Tools → Power plan): the supply (venue or generator, single- or three-phase, amps per phase, 230/400 V 50 Hz by default), your distros and their outputs, and every load plugged in. It adds up the current on each phase and each output, the neutral, how close each breaker is to its limit (a warning from 80%), how far apart the phases are, and suggests a generator size with a margin.
- Loads come in by themselves from the light plot and DMX patch, the sound system and the gig's equipment; catering, bars or a site office can be typed in by hand with their watts.
- Each distro and load shows whether it is connected, partly connected or not connected, in words and a sign, not by colour alone.
- A printable **power report**, and "Save to gig files" puts it as a PDF in the Files tab. Everything is labelled as an estimate for a qualified electrician.
- **Electrical fields** on catalogue items: typical power, power factor, inrush, the amps a datasheet gives (with their voltage) when it gives no watts, connectors in and out, and for distros their inputs and outputs (connector, phase, breaker, RCD).
### Connected
- The light plot's power section (and its print) shows the fixtures' figures from the power plan once the gig has one.
- On-site mode has a Power plan tile (the report, read-only, also offline).
- The import wizard guesses the electrical columns, reads connectors as rental lists write them ("CEE 32/3", "CEE 16A blue") and refuses impossible values.
- Help: the "Plan the power" guide, terms (distro, breaker headroom, neutral current, power factor) and ⓘ hints.

## Pending: new questions (section 3)
- **D77 (amps instead of watts):** chosen: a datasheet's amps are typed with the voltage they're given at and counted as amps × volts (VA, power factor 1); no watt figure is ever made up. Alternative: also ask for a power factor for those. Assumed: as built.
- **Power factor unknown:** counted as 1 (the least current); the report says so. Alternative: a cautious default such as 0.9. Assumed: 1, labelled.
- **Phase balance warning:** at 20% apart (an assumption, not from a standard). Is another figure preferred?
- **Generator sizes:** the suggestion rounds up to common rental sizes (10 … 2000 kVA, prime) with a 25% margin by default (editable per gig). Should it use your suppliers' actual sizes?
- **IM1 fix suggestions (like D80):** new import refusals `powerRange`, `powerNeedVolts`, `powerTypical` (edit the cell; for `powerNeedVolts` also "fill the voltage for all rows: 230") and the database's `powerSpec` (edit the cell); `choice` on a connector column could offer the connector list.
- **Distros by CSV:** a distro's outputs can't be imported from a file (they're typed in the item form). Wanted?
