# V3: packing list per vehicle (step 23, part 3)

## Changelog
### Added
- **Packing list per vehicle** (gig → Transport → "Packing list per vehicle", also on the packing list and the scan screen): which cases and items ride in which vehicle on the gig's outward trips. Each vehicle shows its weight and volume against its maximum load and load space, with an "Over capacity" mark and the overload in words. Planners move any number of a line between vehicles, or back to "Not in a vehicle yet".
- **Suggest a split**: AVX spreads the packing list across the vehicles, heaviest and bulkiest first, each into the vehicle that stays least full, keeping cases whole. You see the split, and any vehicle that ends up over, before applying it.
- **Unknown, never zero**: items with no weight or no volume are counted and listed apart ("+ 3 without weight"), so a missing figure never makes a vehicle look emptier.
- **Volume on items** (Edit item → Volume (m³)). If it's empty, AVX reads the item's dimensions when they have three numbers and a unit ("60 × 40 × 35 cm").
- **Scan out per vehicle**: the scan screen has a Vehicle picker; the list then shows only what goes in that vehicle, cases' contents included.
- Help: term "Packing list per vehicle"; ⓘ hints on the item's volume, the suggested split and unknown figures; guide "Split the packing list across vehicles".

### Connected
- Packing list (a "Per vehicle" button), scanning out (the Vehicle picker), the gig Transport tab (a link under the trips), item weights (and the new volume) from Inventory, cases and their contents (kits).
- Not connected: the scan log doesn't record which vehicle a scan was for (counts on the scan screen stay per gig); full-screen scan mode has no vehicle picker yet.
