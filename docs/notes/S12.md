# S12: Rigging RG1 — weights, hang points and hoists (step 17a)

## Changelog
#### Added
- **Rigging tool** (Gig → Tools → Rigging): truss lines with their load — fixtures and weights from
  the light plot, the truss's own weight, a cable allowance per metre, accessories, extra loads
  (at one place or spread) and motors that climb with the line — and what each hang point carries,
  worked out for a continuous truss on two or more points (uneven loads and overhangs included).
  Each point is checked against its hoist's WLL after a dynamic factor, and a D8 hoist over people
  is flagged. Everything is labelled as an aid that a competent rigger must check.
- **Hoist models:** Chainmaster D8+ 500, 1000 and 2000 kg from the manufacturer's data sheets (source
  and revision shown); CM Lodestar D8+ 0.5, 1 and 2 t listed without values (their data sheet
  couldn't be reached). Admins and members add their own from a hoist's data plate. Every model
  shows "needs a second person's check before use".
- Help: the guide "Work out the rigging loads", terms (WLL, hoist class, dynamic factor, hang
  point) and ⓘ hints.

#### Connected
- **Light plot:** bars with weights show "Rigging: not checked", "checked" (with the busiest
  hoist's share of its WLL) or "overloaded"; one tap opens the rigging tool on that line. Changing
  the fixtures makes it "not checked" again.
- **Tools tab:** suggests the rigging tool when hoists are in the gig's equipment.

#### For developers
- `20270107000000_rigging.sql`: `gig_show.rigging` (jsonb); table `hoist_models` with RLS
  (presets read by everyone signed in; an organisation's rows read by its members, written by
  admins and members; removed by marking) and trigger `private.hoist_models_before`; six presets.
- The tool id `rigging` joins `organisations.field_tools` and `gig_tools.tool` through S09's
  hand-run `20270106000001_tool_ids_power_rigging.sql` (already run on test).
- Solver: `src/lib/rigging.js` (`solveBeam`, `analyseLine`, `barRigging`); tests
  `src/lib/rigging.test.js` against hand calculations; RLS test `supabase/tests/rigging.sql`.

## Pending: test lines (section 2)
- Light plot with fixtures (with weights) on a truss: the bar shows "Rigging: not checked"; tap it:
  the rigging tool opens with that line, fixtures counted, two hang points.
- Choose a hoist per point: each shows its load, the load × the dynamic factor and the % of WLL;
  make one overloaded (heavy extra load) and check it turns red with words, and the light plot says
  "Rigging: overloaded".
- Put a point in the middle of a three-point line and a heavy load on an overhang: the far point
  says it would be pulled up.
- A D8 hoist (add one from a data plate) with "Over people" ticked is flagged.
- Mark the loads as checked; move a fixture in the light plot: the bar says "not checked" again.

  suggests the rigging tool.

  them.

## Pending: new questions (section 3)
- **Default cable allowance: 5 kg/m.** No standard gives one; 5 kg/m is the top of the 1.5–5.2 kg/m
  range a cable-weight calculator quotes for looms tied along a span
  (cmuse.org/cable-weight-total-calculator) — a conservative start. Editable per gig and per
  line; labelled in the app as a default to check. Confirm or give your own.
- **Default dynamic factor: 1.2.** The low end of the 1.2–1.6 "usually" added to live loads when
  hoists are used (Runtemund Engineering, "Rigging dynamic factors", 22 Oct 2021,
  runtemund.de/en/rigging-dynamic-factors-in-t-d-s). A safer default would be higher (1.5 is used
  in their worked example). Editable per gig; labelled as a default to check. Confirm or change.
- **Hoist presets need a second person's check before use** (like truss tables): Chainmaster D8+
  500 kg (DS_CH_D8PLUS_SK_030-76U…, rev 1.1, 18.10.2024), 1000 kg (DS_CH_D8PLUS_SK_070-76U…, rev
  1.1, 18.10.2024), 2000 kg 2-fall (DS_CH_D8PLUS_SK_070-78U…, rev 1.1, 02.06.2025), all from
  chainmaster.de (URLs on each preset). Who checks them, and should RG4's sign-off cover them?
- **CM Lodestar D8+ 0.5/1/2 t have no values:** cmco.com's Lodestar D8+ product pages return 404
  and no CM-hosted D8+ data sheet was found. Columbus McKinnon's own Northern Ireland site has a
  2022 Lodestar catalogue extract, but its table doesn't say which class the ratings are for, so it
  wasn't used. Can you get the D8+ data sheet so they can be filled? Ask Columbus McKinnon's entertainment team
  (CM-ET) through the contact page at cmco.com → Industries → Entertainment → Contact
  (www.cmco.com/en-gb/industries/entertainment/contact/), or your CM supplier; until then add your
  own model from the hoist's data plate.
- **"Motor climbs with the line"** adds the hoist's own weight (without chain) at that point. Chain
  weight hanging from a fixed (body-up) hoist isn't counted. Should RG4 add it?
- The tool assumes all points at the same height and rigid; uneven trims or soft points (bridles)
  come with RG2.
