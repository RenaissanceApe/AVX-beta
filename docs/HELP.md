# Help and inclusion: plan

Status: **H1 built (0.29.0)**, **H2a built (0.30.0)**; H2b–H6 planned. A project of its own, built in stages H1–H6, and a
rule for every feature after it.

The aim: anyone who works in events can use AVX without help from a colleague, whatever their
age, eyesight, language or comfort with technology. Crew are often older, often on a phone,
often outdoors or backstage in the dark, sometimes with gloves on. Many will open AVX a few
times a month. The app has to explain itself in the moment, in plain words.

## Principles

1. **Help where you are, not in a manual.** Explanations sit next to the thing they explain
   (a field, a button, an empty page). A separate help section exists for reading ahead,
   never as the only place.
2. **Plain language first.** Short sentences, everyday words, one idea per sentence. When a
   trade word is the right word ("call time", "PAT test", "cross-hire"), it links to the glossary.
3. **Both languages, always.** Every hint, term and guide exists in Portuguese and English;
   the tests fail otherwise, as they already do for the rest of the app.
4. **Never colour or hover alone.** Status always has a word or icon too. Hints open on tap
   and with the keyboard, not only on mouse hover (phones have no hover).
5. **Screenshots that can't go out of date.** Guide images are generated by a script from the
   real screens (with sample data), in both languages, so every release can refresh them.
6. **Part of "done".** A feature isn't finished until its hints, glossary terms and guide are.

## What gets built

### Glossary
- A searchable page (**Help → Glossary**) with every AVX and trade term, each with a one-line
  definition, an example and where it appears in the app.
- Terms in the interface get a dotted underline; tapping one shows its definition in place.
- Starting list (about 50): gig, production manager, crew, viewer, organisation roles, workspace,
  call time, shift, booking, double booking, availability request, option/hold vs confirmed,
  prep and return days, catalogue item, bulk vs serial-numbered, unit, location, stock movement,
  stock count/correction, set, case, accessory, alternative, packing list, scan out/in, damaged,
  repair, write-off, inspection (PAT), book value, depreciation, subrental, supplier, cross-hire
  code, hire request, quote, quote version, phase, section, VAT, validity, client link, call
  sheet, feed, chat/channel, direct message, mute, unread, email summary, two-step sign-in,
  offline copy, your network, who can find you.

### Field hints
- An **ⓘ** next to fields that are ambiguous or have consequences. It opens a short explanation
  with an example, e.g. *Prep days: "How many days before the gig the gear is booked, for loading
  and testing. Example: 1 = the gear is unavailable from the day before."*
- First batch (the fields people get wrong): prep/return days, tracking (bulk vs serial),
  kit type (set vs case), minimum stock, replacement value, depreciation months, inspection
  interval, unit status, subrental status, cross-hire code, quote phases and factors, discount,
  VAT, validity, call time vs shift, gig status, organisation roles, gig roles, "who can find
  you", email summary, two-step sign-in, offline files, mark lost, write-off.

### Guides ("how to")
- **Help** in the menu, plus a **?** on every page header that opens that page's guide in a side
  panel, so you don't lose your place.
- Each guide: what it's for, numbered steps with a screenshot each (the relevant button
  outlined), common mistakes, related terms. Written per role:
  - **Crew:** accept an invite and set a password; install AVX on your phone; find your call
    time and the call sheet; mark unavailable dates; answer an availability question; chat;
    report damage from site; use AVX without signal.
  - **Production managers:** create a gig; invite crew (and from your network); schedule;
    call sheet; double bookings; plan equipment; the gig chat.
  - **Warehouse:** import the catalogue; locations; units and QR labels; scan out/in; returns
    and damage; repairs and inspections; subrentals and cross-hire; reports.
  - **Office / sales:** company details; price list; build a quote with phases; versions; send
    to the client; from accepted quote to gig.
  - **Admins:** organisation members and roles; muting; deleting safely; exporting data.
- Screenshots come from a Playwright script (like the visual checks run on every phase), in
  Portuguese and English, phone and desktop, stored with the app.

### Teaching empty states and first steps
- An empty page explains what goes there and offers the first action ("No items yet. Paste
  your list from a spreadsheet, or add one item.").
- A **first-steps checklist** per role on the home page until it's done (admin: company details,
  catalogue, invite people; crew: name and phone, install on phone, notifications).

## More inclusive features (suggested)

| Feature | Who it helps |
|---|---|
| **Text size** setting (normal, large, extra large) that the whole layout follows, separate from browser zoom | Low vision, older eyes, small phones |
| **Comfortable mode**: bigger buttons and spacing (touch targets at least 44 px) | Shaky hands, gloves, older users |
| **Light theme and high-contrast theme** (today AVX is dark only) | Outdoor loading bays in sunlight; low vision |
| **Readable font option** (Atkinson Hyperlegible, made for low vision, free) | Low vision, dyslexia |
| **Simple mode for crew**: home shows only next gig, call time, call sheet, chat and "report a problem" | Occasional users, low confidence with apps |
| **Field kit for scanning**: huge buttons, sound and vibration on each scan, torch button, works one-handed | Warehouse in the dark, gloves, noise |
| **Read aloud** the call sheet and messages (the phone's built-in voice); dictation already works in every text box | Low vision, reading difficulty, hands busy |
| **Large-print call sheet** option | Older crew, printed sheets on site |
| **Accessibility checks in CI** (axe) so screen-reader and keyboard problems fail the build | Blind and keyboard-only users |
| **Undo** after common actions, not only warnings before | Everyone; fewer frightening mistakes |
| **Ask for help** button that messages your organisation's admin with the page you're on | People who won't read a guide |
| **More languages** (Spanish and French first, for the international job board) | Crew abroad, non-native speakers |
| **SMS or WhatsApp** for call-time changes, for people who never open apps (paid per message) | People who live on SMS/WhatsApp |
| **Plain-language review** of every existing text, once | Everyone |

## What H1 built (0.29.0)

- **Help content** in `src/help/en.js` and `src/help/pt.js` (the same ids in both): 64 glossary
  terms (term, definition, example, where it appears), 23 field hints and 8 page notes.
  `src/help/help.test.js` fails if a language is missing anything, a text is empty, or the app
  uses a hint, term or page that doesn't exist.
- **Help → Glossary** (`/help`, readable signed out too): search (accents and case don't matter),
  language switch, and `/help#call-time` opens at that word. In the top bar on computers (?) and
  in **More** on phones.
- **Components** (`src/components/help/HelpParts.jsx`): `<Hint id>` (ⓘ, opens on tap or keyboard,
  closes with Escape, stays inside the screen), `<HintLabel htmlFor hint>` (a label with its ⓘ),
  `<Term id>` (a word with a dotted underline that shows its definition and links to the
  glossary) and `<PageHelp page>` (the ? on a page header: what the page is for and the words used
  on it, in a side panel).
- **Hints on the first fields:** tracking, kit type, minimum stock, replacement value, inspection
  interval, depreciation (two places), unit status, prep and return days, who can find you,
  two-step sign-in, phase discount, factor, VAT (two places), validity, gig access, call time,
  gig status, organisation role, mark as lost, cross-hire code. Terms in the booking window.
- **? on page headers:** gigs, gig, messages, inventory, an item, quotes, a quote, organisations.
- **Accessibility checks:** axe (`@axe-core/playwright`) in the browser tests on the public pages
  and, in the signed-in tests, the organisation and gig pages; serious and critical findings
  fail the build. Fixes made: the muted grey (`#4A6070`, about 3:1) became `#7D93A9` (at least
  4.5:1 on every background it's used on); empty-state text, light blue, pink and purple
  lightened; the logo, account and company buttons are now named by what they show (voice
  control).
- Not yet (H2): guides with screenshots; hints on the remaining fields.

## What H2a built (0.30.0)

- **Guide format** (`src/help/index.js` → `GUIDES`): for each guide, who it's for (`crew`, `pm`),
  the pages whose **?** lists it, the glossary words it links to and one screenshot name per step
  (or none). The words (`title`, `intro`, `steps`, `mistakes`) are in `src/help/en.js` and
  `pt.js` under `guides`. `help.test.js` fails if a language is missing a guide, the step counts
  differ, a word or page doesn't exist, or a screenshot is missing in either language.
- **14 guides.** Crew: accept an invitation and set a password; install AVX on your phone; find
  your call time and the call sheet; mark the dates you can't work; answer an availability
  question; chat with the team; report damaged equipment; use AVX without signal. Production
  managers: create a gig; invite crew; build the schedule; prepare and send the call sheet; avoid
  double bookings; plan the equipment. ("The gig chat" from the plan is covered by the crew chat
  guide.)
- **Where they show:** Help (`/help`) lists them by role above the glossary; each has its own
  address (`/help/guide/crew-call-time`). The **?** on a page header lists that page's guides,
  grouped by role, and opens them inside the panel, so you don't lose your place.
- **Screenshots** (`npm run help:screens`, in `scripts/help-screens/`): builds the app against a
  pretend database address, answers every call with sample data (`sample-data.mjs`: Carla, crew
  only, and Ricardo, admin and production manager; dates counted from today), opens each screen
  on a 390 px phone, outlines in yellow what to tap and saves a JPEG per language in
  `public/help/shots/<pt|en>/` (21 per language, about 4 MB in all; not cached for offline use).
  Date and time fields follow each language. A failed scene saves what the screen looked like in
  `node_modules/.help-screens-failed/`. The pictures have no alt text on purpose: each step's
  text says everything, the picture only shows where.
- **Found while taking them, fixed:** on a phone, the "Add schedule item" and "New gig" forms
  were wider than the screen (date and time fields side by side); the "New gig" labels weren't
  linked to their fields (screen readers read them without a name); the invite form's email
  example was in English in Portuguese.
- Not yet (H2b): guides for the warehouse, office and admins.

## The rule for every new feature (from H1 on)

A feature's PR also ships:
- glossary entries for any new term, in both languages;
- ⓘ hints on any field that is ambiguous or has consequences;
- an update to the guide for that page, with refreshed screenshots;
- a keyboard and screen-reader pass (the axe checks must pass);
- text reviewed for plain language.
